About Us

St Paul Methodist School Timor-Leste

Who We Are

St Paul Methodist School Timor-Leste is a Christian educational institution providing quality education from Primary School through Senior High School. We are committed to nurturing the whole child — academically, spiritually, socially, and emotionally.

Our school community welcomes students from diverse backgrounds, united by a shared commitment to excellence, integrity, and service.
